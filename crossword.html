      💡 Getting the Most Out of the Crossword

      Every clue is a definition, so a crossword is retrieval practice: try to recall the term from the clue before you lean on the crossing letters, and use the crossings to confirm or correct yourself. Save Check Puzzle for after your first full pass, since checking after every word removes the struggle that makes the practice work. Teachers can print the puzzle and a separate answer key, or link students to one specific puzzle by adding ?set=u1s1 (using that puzzle's ID) to this page's address.
