💡 Getting the Most Out of Today's Challenge

Try to hold off on the hint button for at least 10–15 seconds of genuine recall before tapping it: revealing it early is an easy habit to fall into, but it forfeits the +5 Perfect Recall bonus every time. The five modes reward different skills, so play to them: Hangman and Anagram Scramble reward accuracy, Fact or Fiction and The Distractor reward speed (a couple of badges specifically require answering in 2 seconds or less), and Synapse Snap rewards memory without peeking. And if you've already finished today's puzzle, don't feel locked out, Practice Mode on any day within the last 90 days carries zero pressure, which makes it a genuinely good way to actually learn a term you missed instead of just moving past it.
