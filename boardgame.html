🧠 Psych Land

Expedition Briefing

Rules of the Road: Select your player count and review unit. On your turn, click "Draw Card" to roll for 1-5 spaces. You will be prompted with a psychology definition—select the correct term to move forward! If you answer incorrectly, you stay put and lose your turn. Watch out for Synaptic Gaps (⚡) that surge you forward and Brain Freezes (❄️) that make you skip a turn. First to the end wins!

🎮 Play Modes: Test your knowledge solo by flying past an automated computer bot, or select 2-4 players to face off against your classmates using a single device—just pass the computer around when your turn pops up!
