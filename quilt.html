💡 Getting the Most Out of Piece of Mind

Piece of Mind is a quilt-style matching game that uses the same term bank as the other review tools, so every finished quilt doubles as a quick pass through real vocabulary. Start in Guided mode to learn how the edges work, then move to Standard once you can place tiles without hints, and try Advanced when you want the extra challenge of rotating them. Short text is great for speed, while Long text adds the extra detail that often decides between two similar terms. On a phone, the Small board is the easiest to read; use the zoom buttons or the Selected tile panel to read tight text, and drag the tile from that panel onto the board if you like. When you finish, read the study list at the bottom: every pair on your quilt links to its topic, so the terms you hesitated on are easy to review.
