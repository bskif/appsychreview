💡 Getting the Most Out of the Daily Challenge

The daily habit is where this page really earns its keep — each puzzle only takes a minute or two, but showing up for it consistently is what builds streaks and, more importantly, keeps low-stakes retrieval practice part of your routine instead of only cramming before a test. Puzzles roughly follow the pace of a typical AP Psych school year, moving through units in order as the year progresses and mixing in review questions over breaks and summer, so what you see here should generally line up with what you're covering in class. Miss a day or fall behind? The Practice Archive below lets you replay any puzzle from the last 90 days in Practice Mode; it won't count for streak or points, but the review value is exactly the same. Don't skip past the Trophy Case tooltips either — every badge is actually named after a real psychology concept (Miller's Law, Long-Term Potentiation, Signal Detection Theory, and more), so hovering to read what each one references is a quick, painless way to pick up extra vocabulary along the way.

🏆 Trophy Case

Miller's Law
Awarded for maintaining a 7-day daily puzzle playing habit. This honors George Miller's discovery that human short-term memory capacity is typically limited to holding 7 ± 2 items at a time.
LTP
Unlocked after achieving a massive 14-day daily winning streak. It connects to the neuroscientific process where repetitive cellular activity permanently strengthens the synaptic pathways between neurons.
Continuous Reinf.
Awarded for completing a solid month of 30 consecutive days of site activity. This represents B.F. Skinner’s operant conditioning schedule where a target behavior is rewarded every single time it occurs.
Absolute Threshold
Earned upon reaching your first 50 total accumulated points. It references the psychological boundary where a faint stimulus transitions from imperceptible to actively detectable at least 50% of the time.
Crystallized Intel.
Earned upon reaching a cumulative lifetime score of 250 total points. This connects to Raymond Cattell's concept of cognitive knowledge, vocabulary, and skills accumulated through a lifetime.
Self-Actualization
Unlocked when you reach the ultimate milestone of 500 total points. It represents the absolute pinnacle of Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs, where an individual fulfills their highest psychological potential.
Action Potential
Earned by solving any daily puzzle in under 15 seconds. This rapid processing speed mirrors the brief, all-or-nothing electrical impulse that fires down an axon to transmit neural data.
Automatic Process
Earned by accumulating 10 speed bonuses over your historical gameplay. This references the cognitive phenomenon of effortlessly and unconsciously encoding everyday information without active mental strain.
Distributed Practice
Awarded for playing an archive game on the same day you complete a daily challenge. This utilizes the spacing effect, a proven learning principle showing that breaking up practice over time vastly improves retention.
Broca's Champion
Earned by successfully solving an Anagram Scramble puzzle of 10 or more letters without making a mistake. It honors Broca's area, dedicated to language assembly and fluent speech production.
Signal Detection
Earned by correctly solving a Fact or Fiction puzzle in 2 seconds or less. This connects to Signal Detection Theory, which explains how the mind separates meaningful target stimuli from confusing background noise.
Phineas Gage
Awarded for winning a game of Psych-Man while down to your very last remaining brain part. This commemorates psychology's most famous medical history anomaly regarding frontal lobe damage.
Selective Attention
Earned by correctly solving a Distractor puzzle in 2 seconds or less. This represents the mental faculty required to lock onto a single stream of information while filtering out irrelevant environmental noise.
Hippocampal Hero
Earned by completing a game of Synapse Snap with one or fewer mismatched flips. It is named after the hippocampus, the critical brain structure responsible for processing explicit long-term memories.
