💡 Getting the Most Out of Connections

Connections is less about recalling a definition and more about recognizing how concepts relate to each other, which lines up well with how AP Psych actually tests you, since the exam often asks you to distinguish between similar-sounding terms rather than define them in isolation. Filter by unit while a topic is still fresh so the puzzle stays tied to what you're currently covering, then try "Mixed Units" once you want a tougher, more exam-realistic challenge. Pay attention to the distractors designed to fit more than one category; figuring out why a term doesn't belong is usually more useful than solving the easy group first. And if you get a "one away," treat it as information about which term you're misreading, not just a wrong guess.
