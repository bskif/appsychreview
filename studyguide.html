💡 Getting the Most Out of the Study Guides

Think of each unit card here as a table of contents, not a replacement for the topic pages themselves: the real content lives behind each link. Work through a unit's topics in order the first time through, since later ones often build on earlier ones (Unit 2's memory topics, for instance, only really click once encoding is covered). Once you've read a topic page, the terms bolded throughout it are the same ones waiting in the Glossary and drilled in Flashcards, Battleship, and Psych Land, so a solid workflow is read, then reinforce with one of those tools, rather than saving all your review for the week before the exam.
