1.Information We Do and Do Not Collect

Psych Review Hub has no accounts, logins, or sign-up forms. We do not ask for, and do not store, your name, email address, school, age, or any other personal information. We do not sell information, and we do not build profiles of individual visitors.

A few outside services that load on our pages (listed in Section 5) may receive information from your browser, such as your IP address, when they deliver ads, fonts, or videos. Their use of that information is covered by their own privacy policies.

If you contact us through our feedback form, the form is hosted by Google Forms, so what you type is collected by Google Forms and shared with us. We use it only to answer you and to fix errors. Please do not include personal details about yourself or other students.

2.Website Analytics

We use Simple Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site. Simple Analytics is a privacy-first tool that does not use cookies and does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). It reports general information, such as which pages are visited, the type of device and browser, and the general location of visits. We use this data strictly to improve our educational tools and website performance.

3.Advertising and Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements, which helps keep our educational resources free for students. Please be aware of the following regarding ad delivery:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

The pages of Psych Review Hub do not set cookies of their own. The advertising cookies described here are set by Google and its advertising partners. To learn more about how Google handles data, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4.Local Storage and Game Progress

Some features save your progress using your browser's local storage (localStorage):

  • The Daily Puzzle saves your cumulative points, game win/loss history, and daily streaks.
  • Piece of Mind saves your best completion times and your game settings.

This data is saved entirely on your own device. It is never sent to our servers, and we do not share it with anyone. Clearing your browser cache or site data will reset these statistics. All of our other tools, including the quizzes, flashcards, and word games, keep your progress only while the page is open.

6.External Links

Our website may contain links to external sites that are not operated by us. Please be aware that we have no control over the content and policies of those sites, and cannot accept responsibility or liability for their respective privacy practices.

7.Children's Privacy

Psych Review Hub is designed for high school and college-level students studying AP Psychology and is not directed at or intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

8.Your Choices

  • Ads: Opt out of personalized advertising using Google's Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info (see Section 3).
  • Cookies: Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. The study tools and games work without them.
  • Saved progress: Clear your site data in your browser's settings to delete everything the Daily Puzzle and Piece of Mind have saved on your device.
  • Questions or removal requests: Reach out through the feedback form below and we will respond.

9.Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10.Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our feedback form.
