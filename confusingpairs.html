💡 Getting the Most Out of Confusing Pairs

This game exists because some of the most commonly mixed-up terms on the AP exam look similar on paper but mean something different in context — that's exactly what each drop zone is testing you on. Filter by unit while you're still building initial understanding, then switch to "All Units" once you want practice untangling terms across categories, since cross-unit mix-ups are often where students actually lose points. If a set keeps tripping you up, try reading every definition in the pool first and predicting which term each one matches before you start dragging — getting it right on the first try matters far less than being able to explain why the wording points to one term over the other.
