💡 Getting the Most Out of Flashcards

Use Mark as Known aggressively: the goal is to shrink your deck down to the terms you're actually struggling with, not to flip through everything on autopilot. Try switching to Definition First mode occasionally too; being handed a definition and recalling the term is closer to how AP Psych questions actually work than the reverse. Every term here is also the same one bolded the first time it appears in our Topic Overview pages, so if a card isn't sticking, it's worth reading that term in context there rather than just re-drilling the definition in isolation. And as you get closer to the exam, study by mixing units together instead of one at a time, since interleaved practice like that is consistently shown to build stronger long-term recall than reviewing a single unit in isolation.
